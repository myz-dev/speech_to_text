Hold ⌃⌥Space to dictate, or tap it to start and stop when you stop speaking.

Settings

On: You speak, then the text is typed into the app you are using, by pressing ⌘V for you. This needs the Accessibility permission (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility); macOS asks for it at the first paste.
Off: the text is only put on the clipboard, and you paste it yourself with ⌘V.
